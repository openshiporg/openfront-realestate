## Blocker

### QA-001 · dashboard · The QA admin still gets "Access Denied" on every dashboard route (third round)
- URL: https://openfront-real-estate-production.up.railway.app/dashboard/signin
- Steps: Sign in with the `realestate` credentials. Then open `/dashboard`, `/dashboard/platform/store-settings` or `/dashboard/init`.
- Expected: The dashboard home with its sidebar.
- Actual: Every route redirects to `/dashboard/no-access` ("Access Denied … contact your administrator"). `authenticatedItem` returns `role: null`, `activeBrokerage: null` and `brokerageMemberships: []` for user `c2806bca129936b531c9327ae`. `logins.json` notes that this user was inserted by psql without `activeBrokerage` or a membership.
- Fix:
  - **Data/Euler:** give the user an active owner/admin `BrokerageMembership` in the QA brokerage and set `activeBrokerage`, or create the admin through the app's own init or invite flow.
  - **Product (`features/keystone/access.ts` `activeBrokerageIds`, and `app/dashboard/no-access/page.tsx`):** a dashboard-role user with no membership should be told so, not sent to "contact your administrator".
- Impact: The whole dashboard, onboarding, Store Settings, and the lead → showing → deal workflow are still **unverified after three rounds**.

### QA-002 · storefront · The rental listing can't take any lead: "Request a showing" and "Ask about applying" both fail
- URL: https://openfront-real-estate-production.up.railway.app/listings/eastside-townhome-rental
- Steps:
  1. Ask about applying: signed in as QA R3 Customer, enter a message and click Send message. Repeat while signed out, with a name and `qa.r3.anon.q2@example.test`.
  2. Tour: signed out, choose Thu Nov 19 2:00 PM and enter a name, an email and notes. Click Request showing.
- Expected: "Message sent…" and "Request sent…", as on the two for-sale listings.
- Actual:
  1. Both question attempts show "We could not send your message. Please try again." The server action got `success: false`, which means the GraphQL call threw.
  2. The tour shows "We could not confirm your request was sent. Please contact us before trying again."
  - The same steps on *Hill Country Estate* succeed, both anonymous and signed in. So the failure is specific to this listing, which is the only `listingType: rent` and the only `status: coming_soon` listing. That makes the brokerage's only rental a dead end: every renter who asks or books is lost.
- Likely files: `features/keystone/mutations/submitListingInquiry.ts` and `requestPropertyShowing.ts`. Both run `lockPropertyForDecision` and then `capturePublicLead` / `LeadActivity.createOne` / `enqueueDomainEvent` *outside* the `requireListingAuthority` try/catch, so any throw becomes a generic GraphQL error. Look for what differs for this row. Candidates:
  - The rental or `coming_soon` path in `requireListingAuthority` / `features/keystone/lib/authorityEvidence.ts`.
  - The new `leaseTerm` / `securityDeposit` / `petPolicy` columns from `20261023180000_real_estate_rental_terms_saved_search_type`. Check that the migration actually ran in production.
  - A lead-routing rule with `intent: rent`.

  The Railway logs for these requests (about 2026-10-23 18:00–18:30 CDT) will show the thrown error. Also, the storefront should show the server's message instead of a generic failure.

### QA-003 · storefront · Showing hours are enforced only in the visitor's own time zone, never on the server, and the form doesn't say which time zone it uses
- URL: /listings/hill-country-estate → Tour
- Steps: In a browser set to Europe/London, request Fri Nov 20 at 09:00, with a name and an email.
- Expected: The time is read in the brokerage's time zone (Austin, CST), or the form shows the zone, and the server refuses anything outside showing hours.
- Actual: "Request sent." The request is stored for **3:00 AM CST**, inside the "8 AM to 8 PM" hint the form shows.
  - `timeProblem()` in `features/storefront/screens/ShowingRequestForm.tsx:20-28` checks `getHours()` in the visitor's zone.
  - `requestPropertyShowing.ts` checks only "in the future, and within a year".
  - Relocating and out-of-state buyers are a normal real-estate customer, so advisors will get middle-of-the-night requests. A direct GraphQL call can skip the browser check entirely.
- Likely files: `ShowingRequestForm.tsx` (label the field "Austin time (CST)" and build the ISO time in the brokerage's zone), `features/keystone/mutations/requestPropertyShowing.ts` (enforce hours server-side, ideally from `BrokerageSettings`), and `features/lib/date-time.ts`.
