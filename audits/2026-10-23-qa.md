## Blocker

### QA-001 · dashboard · The QA admin signs in but gets "Access Denied" on every dashboard route
- URL: https://openfront-real-estate-production.up.railway.app/dashboard/signin
- Steps: Sign in with the `realestate` admin credentials.
- Expected: The dashboard home loads, with the sidebar.
- Actual: Sign-in succeeds, then `/dashboard`, `/dashboard/platform/listings` and `/dashboard/platform/store-settings` all redirect to `/dashboard/no-access` ("Access Denied … contact your administrator"). `logins.json` records an older symptom: `authenticatedItem` was null and the dashboard redirected back to sign-in.
- Cause: The QA User row was inserted with psql and has no `activeBrokerage`. `canAccessDashboard` needs `activeBrokerageIds(session).length > 0` (`features/keystone/access.ts:37`, `:141`). `sessionData` loads `activeBrokerage` (`features/keystone/index.ts:75`).
- Fix: Mostly a data/setup fix. Give the QA admin a membership and an `activeBrokerage`, or create the admin through the app's own signup or init. Product side: a user with `canAccessDashboard` and no active brokerage should be told why and offered a way to pick a brokerage, not shown a generic "contact your administrator" page. Dashboard QA has to be re-run once this is fixed.

## Major

### QA-002 · storefront · Text is invisible on every primary button that is a link
- URLs: Every storefront page. Affected buttons: the header **Contact** (desktop), the home **Contact an advisor**, the mobile sticky **Request a showing** on listing pages, **Browse homes** in the empty My showings state, **Clear search** in the no-results state, and pagination **Next**.
- Steps: Open `/` at 1440px and look at the button at the top right of the header. Or open `/listings/hill-country-estate` at 390px and scroll down.
- Expected: Light text on a dark button.
- Actual: The button is a solid black pill with no visible label. The computed `color` is the same as the `background` (`oklch(0.21 0.012 151)`). `<button>` primaries such as **Search** and **Request showing** look correct.
- Cause: `.storefront-root a { color: inherit }` (`features/storefront/styles/storefront.css:9`, specificity 0-1-1) beats `.sf-btn--primary { color: var(--sf-paper) }` (`:35`, 0-1-0). Fix by raising the specificity of the button rule, for example `.storefront-root .sf-btn--primary` or `a.sf-btn--primary`, or by wrapping the base `a` rule in `:where()`.

### QA-003 · storefront · My showings shows the customer's requested time in UTC
- URL: /account
- Steps: Signed in, with the browser in America/Chicago, request a showing for Nov 5 at 5:30 PM. Then open My showings.
- Expected: "Thu, Nov 5, 5:30 PM" in the customer's (or the brokerage's) time zone.
- Actual: "Thu, Nov 5, 11:30 PM UTC". A buyer would read that as the wrong time.
- Cause: `features/storefront/screens/AccountPage.tsx:9` uses `timeZone: 'UTC'`, and line 37 appends " UTC". Format in the brokerage's time zone, or on the client in local time (there is already a `LocalDateTime` component in `features/platform/showings/components/`).

### QA-004 · storefront · No "Forgot password" on customer sign-in
- URL: /account
- Steps: Open the sign-in card.
- Expected: A reset link. The dashboard has `/dashboard/reset`.
- Actual: There is no recovery path. A buyer who forgets their password loses their showing history. `customerSignIn` and `AccountForm` have no reset flow (`features/storefront/components/AccountForm.tsx`, `features/storefront/lib/data/account.ts`).

### QA-005 · storefront · Shared listing links point the preview image at localhost, and the image is an SVG
- URL: /listings/hill-country-estate (all listings)
- Steps: `curl` the page and look at `og:image` and `twitter:image`.
- Expected: An absolute production URL to a raster (JPG/PNG) image.
- Actual: `http://localhost:3000/images/real-estate/properties/hill-country-estate.svg`. Listing links shared over text, Facebook or Slack get no preview. Agents share listing links constantly, so this matters.
- Cause: There is no `metadataBase` in `app/(storefront)/layout.tsx` `generateMetadata`. `generateListingMetadata` (`features/storefront/screens/ListingDetailPage.tsx:25`) passes a relative `imagePath`. Social crawlers also ignore SVG.

### QA-006 · dashboard · A signed-in user who is not staff is stuck: no way to sign out or switch accounts
- URL: /dashboard/no-access
- Steps: While signed in as a customer, or as the broken admin from QA-001, visit `/dashboard/signin`.
- Expected: The sign-in form, or a "Sign out" button on the Access Denied page.
- Actual: `/dashboard/signin` redirects to `/dashboard/no-access`, which offers only "Go Back" and "Home". The only way out is to find the storefront's `/account` → Sign out, or to clear cookies.
- Likely files: `features/dashboard/middleware.ts`, `features/platform/auth/lib/redirects.ts`, `app/dashboard/no-access/page.tsx`.

### QA-007 · storefront · Default listing and neighborhood art has text baked in that clashes with the overlay captions
- URLs: / (hero feature card and Neighborhoods), /listings/* (hero image), and advisor avatars.
- Steps: Look at the hero card and the neighborhood tiles at desktop and at 390px.
- Expected: Clean photos, with the overlay caption as the only text.
- Actual: The SVG artwork has its own "Hill Country Estate / WESTLAKE HILLS" label. On the hero card and on every neighborhood tile it sits under, and overlaps, the real caption, giving garbled double text ("Westlake Hills" over "Hill Country Estate", "Rainey Street Historic District" over "Riverfront Loft"). On the listing hero the label is cropped mid-word ("Country Estate / LAKE HILLS"). The advisor avatar (`agents/maya-chen.svg`) shows a tiny card with "Chen" text. A merchant's first impression is broken-looking imagery.
- Likely files: `public/images/real-estate/properties/*.svg`, `public/images/real-estate/agents/*.svg`, `features/lib/real-estate-images.ts`, and the onboarding seed (`features/platform/onboarding/lib/seed.json`). Remove the text from the artwork, or use plain imagery.

### QA-008 · storefront · Business gap: buyers cannot save homes or searches
- URL: /, /listings/*, /account
- Expected: On day one, a buyer expects a heart/save button on listings, a "Saved homes" tab, and a saved search with alerts. These are standard on every brokerage site. The schema already has `SavedProperty`, `SavedSearch` and `SavedSearchAlert` (`features/keystone/models/`).
- Actual: There is no save control anywhere. The account has only "My showings" and "Profile" (`features/storefront/components/AccountTabs.tsx`). There is also no list of the customer's questions/inquiries. Lesser day-one gaps worth tracking: no photo gallery on the listing page (one hero image even when there are several `media`), no map, no sort (price, newest), and no baths filter.

### QA-009 · storefront · No favicon, robots.txt or sitemap.xml
- URLs: /favicon.ico, /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml all return 404.
- Expected: A brokerage depends on search traffic to its listings, so a sitemap of `/listings/*` and a robots file are table stakes. A favicon is needed for tabs and bookmarks.
- Actual: All three return 404, and no `<link rel="icon">` is emitted. There is also no `/listings` index route (404), even though the breadcrumb says "All homes".
- Fix: Add `app/robots.ts`, `app/sitemap.ts` and `app/icon.*` (the E-commerce reference covers this pattern).

## Minor

- Expected: Ignore the invalid filter, or show "Enter a valid price".
- Actual: A red-tone state, "Homes could not be loaded. Try again later.", which looks like an outage. Separately, typing `1,000,000` into Max price is blocked with the browser's generic "Please match the requested format." People type commas and `$` in prices.
